# Monetization Strategy

**Document ID:** SEG-MON-001

**Project:** SendEGram

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Foundation

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term revenue strategy for SendEGram.

The objective is to build sustainable revenue while maintaining user trust and delivering clear customer value.

---

# 2. Monetization Principles

Revenue must never compromise:

* User trust
* Security
* Transparency
* Product quality

Users should always understand why they are paying and what value they receive.

---

# 3. Revenue Streams

## 3.1 International Money Transfers

Revenue may be generated through:

* Transparent transfer fees
* Foreign exchange margin (where applicable and disclosed)
* Partner revenue sharing

All applicable fees must be shown before the user confirms a transaction.

---

## 3.2 Bill Payments

Revenue may come from:

* Service provider commissions
* Partner agreements

Users must never be charged hidden fees.

---

## 3.3 Merchant Services (Future)

Potential revenue sources:

* Merchant transaction fees
* Settlement services
* Business tools

---

## 3.4 Premium Features (Future)

Optional subscription plans may include:

* Advanced financial analytics
* Family financial management
* Multi-account management
* Priority customer support

Core financial services should remain available without requiring a subscription.

---

## 3.5 Business Platform (Future)

Potential services:

* Business accounts
* Payment APIs
* Payroll tools
* Reporting solutions

# 5. Pricing Principles

Pricing must be:

* Transparent
* Competitive
* Easy to understand
* Consistent

Hidden charges are prohibited.

---

# 6. Customer Trust

SendEGram will never:

* Sell personal data.
* Display misleading prices.
* Use dark patterns to increase revenue.
* Charge users without explicit consent.

Trust has higher priority than short-term profit.

---

# 7. Cost Structure

Primary operational costs include:

* Banking and payment partner fees
* Infrastructure and cloud services
* SMS and notification providers
* Identity verification (KYC)
* Customer support
* Security and compliance
* Software development
* Monitoring and observability

---

# 8. Growth Strategy

Initial growth focuses on:

* Uzbek citizens living abroad
* Family-oriented financial services
* Cross-border payments

Future expansion includes:

* Additional countries
* Additional currencies
* Merchant ecosystem
* Business services

---

# 9. Success Metrics

Revenue growth should be evaluated alongside:

* Customer retention
* Transaction volume
* User satisfaction
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)
* Fraud rate
* Average revenue per active user (ARPU)

Revenue alone is not a success metric.

---

# 10. Long-Term Strategy

The long-term objective is to build a diversified financial platform where revenue is generated through multiple complementary services rather than reliance on a single income source.
