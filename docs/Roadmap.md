# Product Roadmap

**Document ID:** SEG-RM-001

**Project:** SendEGram

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Foundation

---

# 1. Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of the SendEGram platform.

It is a strategic planning document, not a delivery guarantee.

Priorities may change based on customer feedback, regulations, technical constraints, and business needs.

---

* MVP Scope
* Competitor Analysis
* User Personas

A complete product foundation ready for engineering.

# Roadmap Principles

The roadmap must remain:

* Flexible
* User-driven
* Data-driven
* Regulation-aware
* Technology-neutral

No feature should be developed solely because it appears on this roadmap.

Every feature must be revalidated before implementation.

---

# Review Policy

This roadmap should be reviewed at least once every quarter.

Priorities may change based on:

* Customer feedback
* Market conditions
* Partner availability
* Regulatory changes
* Technical learnings
